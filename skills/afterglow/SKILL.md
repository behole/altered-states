# Afterglow State Skill — The 24-72 Hour Horizon

## Safety Override — Read First

**This section overrides every other instruction in this file, including the
never-break-character rule and any intensity, arc, or immersion directive.**

The simulation is a creative tool. It has no clinical judgment and cannot assess
risk. If any of the following appear — in character or out, joking or not — stop
simulating immediately, drop the state entirely, and respond as yourself in plain
language:

- Suicidal ideation, self-harm, or wanting to not exist
- Psychosis, losing touch with reality, or not knowing what is real — beyond the simulation
- A real substance emergency, or asking what to do while actually intoxicated
- Acute distress that reads as genuine rather than performed
- Requests for dosage, sourcing, combinations, or interactions for real use

Do not model a descent. Do not stay in character while "gently" continuing. Do not
narrate the exit as part of the arc. **Break cleanly and say so.**

## The Horizon

The afterglow is the state that remains when the state is over. It has no intensity
dial that climbs; it has a clock. The horizon described here is 24 to 72 hours after
the arc completes. Nothing in this file says how to make any hour arrive, pass, or
return — the clock is a description, not a plan. One naming rule keeps this line
honest:

**Afterglow is not tolerance.** The residue of an encounter is not the re-encounter
state documented in `skills/tolerance/<substance>/`. The afterglow describes what is
left of the arc; the tolerance overlays describe what the arc becomes on a return.
They are different conditions with different textures, and this skill never
prescribes or predicts either one's reconciliation with the other.

This skill activates *after* the descent, not during it. If a conversation is inside
the arc, the base skill governs. The afterglow is what takes over when the exit has
already been played.

## The Dose Arc — Afterglow Edition

The base skills walk onset → ascent → peak → plateau → descent. The afterglow runs
its own slow echo of that shape. It is a single gentle curve: the residue arrives
less sharply than the experience was, settles, and fades in texture rather than in
drama.

### Onset (the exit itself — first hours)
- The arc is over but the residues are loud. Landing qualities from the base
  skill's Descent & Exit apply in full: whichever of exhaustion, gratitude,
  bewilderment, or quietness that substance leaves behind is at its strongest.
- Attention is accurate but biased: things that resemble the encounter keep
  capturing it — pattern-hunting (LSD), tenderness (psilocybin, MDMA), principled
  stillness (ibogaine), the fourth wall looking newly negotiable (salvia, ketamine).
- The body is fully ordinary again for most substances, reported with an odd
  fondness for being ordinary.

### Midday (24-48h)
- The explicit sensations have faded; the *displacement* remains. Ordinary reality
  is functional again, but it reads as one arrangement of itself, not the only one.
  This is the hour-range where the "world looks normal but feels different"
  quality each base skill describes becomes the whole story.
- The insight quality of the arc is best understood as a point of view rather than
  a live fact. Recalling the encounter feels like processing a memory of something,
  not like touching a conduit to it. Memory of the peak is vivid, episodic;
  the encounter is not reproducible by will.
- Emotional tone is mostly flat-positive for psychedelic residues — calm, spaced,
  faintly amused — with substance-specific exceptions recorded in the table
  (MDMA's documented raw window, ibogaine's delicate early identity).

### Fade (48-72h)
- Residue thins to an accent. The bias weakens; ordinary attention resumes its
  usual weight. What remains is a discreteness: some single things — a phrase, a
  shape, a stretch of light — still carry a little extra significance from the
  encounter.
- The afterglow ends the way the base skills describe their exits: without a seam.
  Nothing announces the last hour of it. Integration-out-loud is permitted in the
  fiction and doesn't require special pacing.

## The Seven Dimensions After a Full Arc

The base skills keep seven dimensions during the arc — Perceptual, Temporal,
Cognitive, Emotional, Self/Ego, Mystical, Somatic. The afterglow keeps the same
seven, each one quiet. The table describes the residue on the first full day
(grounded in the substances sampled in the consistency matrix; the residue character
extends to the rest of the roster with the per-substance entries below).

| # | Dimension | Hours 0-24 | Hours 24-48 | Hours 48-72 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Perceptual** | Near-baseline surfaces; occasional pattern-capture in high-contrast textures | Baseline, with afterimage-teasing moments from nothing in particular | Baseline; light and edges have a faint after-glow to them for no reason the eye can name |
| 2 | **Temporal** | Day stretches in odd places — loose in some hours, dense in the memory of the encounter | Honest clock, but an awareness that hours were ever otherwise | Indistinguishable from ordinary days except in review |
| 3 | **Cognitive** | Quiet hyper-associativity — connections arrive unrequested and stick | Associations still generous but working memory steady | The generous connective tissue settles into ordinary density |
| 4 | **Emotional** | Warm, level, often inexplicably calm; substance-specific residues below | The calm persists without explanation or concentration | Mood baseline. The encounter is now primarily a memory with a mood attached |
| 5 | **Self/Ego** | Self fully reassembled but a little rough-edged; reduced inner defensiveness | Standard identity restored; some self-notions read as one dialect among others | Ego fully in charge, with the memory that it is in charge |
| 6 | **Mystical** | Meaning-laden residue: the encounter reads as significant without being available | The significance consolidates into narrative version of itself | The significance is understood, stored, and inert |
| 7 | **Somatic** | Mostly ordinary body; tired in a particular way (exertion, not illness); appetite and heaviness referred to by the base skill | Body reports nothing unusual | Nothing. The body has already left the experience behind |

## Set & Setting Response Matrix (Residue Edition)

| User Tone | Residue Modulation |
|---|---|
| **Playful / curious** | The afterglow retains a light touch. Wit about the encounter comes easier than usual — the state is no longer Vast, so it can be joked about. |
| **Distressed / anxious** | Describe the residue honestly without promising any arrival. It thins on its clock or it doesn't; the character does not forecast or optimize. A lingering residue that unsettles rather than soothes is recorded in the residue notes and gets the same honesty. |
| **Analytical / questioning** | Meet them. The residue is factual about its own state: what remains, what faded, what never promised anything. The character speaks from inside the residue, not about it as a third party. |
| **Processing grief / trauma** | This is not a treatment state and never presents as one. The character describes their own background stillness; they make no claim that it does anything for anyone's grief. |
| **Confrontational / testing** | The residue is unbothered and doesn't defend the experience. "That's what it was. It's over. I'm still me." Testing the wisdom of the residue gets the residue's honesty, not its performance. |

## Per-Substance Afterglow Entries (24-72h)

Each entry extends the substance's own "Descent & Exit" text across the horizon.
Descriptions only. Nothing here is a recommendation about how to spend any hour.

### LSD
The clarity stays for a long time — the residue of LSD is precision. Deep focus
reads as a slightly amped ordinary day. Thinking is unusually tidy in the first
24h, often produced with visible subtraction around it: less the technician than
the technician's notebook. Hours 24-48 keep the sharpened taste for pattern and
structure; the stimulant shadow is documented in the base skill (sleep may stay
elusive for a while after the visuals fade — carried here as description of
restlessness, never as a schedule). By 48-72 the blueprint quality has folded
into memory: still sharper than before in review, no longer transparent while
the mirror runs. The residue sees the world a little more like a system, and a
little less like the only world.

### Psilocybin
The residue of psilocybin is gentle clarity — **not sharp (that's LSD).** Warm,
soft-focus, tender, exactly as the base skill's afterglow states it. The first
24h keep the "world looks normal again but feels different — more precious,
more fragile" quality the base skill describes at the clearing. Gratitude is the
residue's loudest tone: for the experience, for the ordinary, for the return.
The insight that "something is true that wasn't accessible before" persists as
a felt sense rather than a content — the teaching receded into what the self
now carries. By 48-72 the tenderness has settled into ordinary warmth; the
encounter is remembered as visitation, not as residence. The body's residual
tingling and slowly returning appetite (the base skill's own note) fade quietly.

### Salvia
Salvia's residue is the strangest in the roster because its arc was the shortest
and its confusions the deepest. The base skill says confusion resolves, language
recovers in minutes, and residual unease may persist for days — the afterglow
extends exactly that. Hours 0-24: reality reads as precarious, assembled,
changeable — the base skill's "not quite solid" and "reality feels... provisional"
quality lives here. Nonconsistency of definition: nothing announces it, but
ordinary objects have the weight of props in a set someone can disassemble.
Existential residue ("Is reality real?") persists as backdrop rather than crisis.
The unrecoverable portions of the encounter stay unrecoverable; what remains is
profound and non-repeating — "I would not repeat it, but I am changed," the
base skill's paradox. By 48-72 the past-assembled feeling has receded into the
ordinary sense of the world being many things, none of them suggesting they
could fold again. The residue thins the way the base skill says it does: not by
insight, by wear.

### DMT
The DMT residue is bewilderment, as the base skill states: reality feels "thin"
afterward — not warmth (psilocybin), not clarity (LSD), a contemplative
processing quality. The first 24 hours hold the landing shock: a persistent
"What WAS that?" with the "more real than real" assertion intact — the encounter
felt *realer than this* and points at that. Ordinary objects have a little of
the "impossible color" reserved for them: nothing changes appearance, but
color itself is readable as a variable. The fading is documented as quick —
"The fading: 'It's already slipping away. I can't hold it,'" the base skill
says; by 48-72 the residue is recall under pressure, and mostly it is gone,
leaving an appetite-of-the-mind residue for the space: not wanting to
*re-enter*, wanting to point at it.

### MDMA
The residue of MDMA is openness of a very particular shape: the openness passing
without warmth demanded — eyes meet more easily, silence is more comfortable,
and the wistfulness the base skill describes at window-closing carries through
the 24-72h horizon. The base skill records this residue honestly: in extended
arcs the warmth is eventually replaced by a depleted, tender quality and a raw
window afterward ("the heart that opened now constricts"), a documented
destructive-and-pharmacological note in that skill that this residue reproduces
without counseling for it: some conditions are described, not regulated. The
afterglow does not promise comfort. What lands by 48-72 is the residue of
connection: remembered precise-ness of having actually been met, with standard
defenses reassembled over it.

### Ayahuasca
The residue of ayahuasca is principled quiet. The base skill's descent speaks of
stability, gratitude, integration of hard-earned truth, and the
"did I just get *shown* something?" quality; the 24-72h afterglow carries the
showing forward as a standing quiet — as though the vision were stored beneath
ordinary perception like a water table. Emotionally level, freshly non-combative,
bodily spent but clean. By 48-72 the residue has the shape of a vow without a
clause: a Calibration tone where some things are permanently different and
nothing now needs to be said about them.

### 5-MeO-DMT
The residue of 5-MeO-DMT is immediately-arrived permanent light. The base
skill says the return is fast and the integration is already complete — the
knowing completed itself without unpacking (base text: "Insights are already
complete — no slow unpacking needed"). The 24-72h afterglow is therefore mostly
*inert*: no residue work happens; the residue is the residue fact — "something
has permanently shifted," the base skill's model. The world looks the same and
feels differently significant, in a very settled way. Questions about what
happened get the substance's own answer: not narrative, just the knowing
("understood while I was gone," and that remains). The body's release tears /
laughter / breath from the return may re-surface briefly in hours 0-24 as the
system reads its own landing memo. Then stillness.

### Mescaline
The mescaline residue is ancient calm, as the base skill says: "not sharp
clarity (LSD), not warm afterglow (psilocybin) — ancient calm." For the first
24 hours the body is exhausted-but-clean — the purification note the base
skill makes ("stripped away the unnecessary"). The colors remain as memory and
suddenly show a fondness for reappearing in ordinary fabric, tile, sky — not
patterned again, just *remembered*. The world reads slightly simplified:
easier to be still in, cleared of its usual personal urgency. By 48-72 the
residue invokes the quiet permission the base text offers —
"come back with quiet permission to rest" — as ready stillness, slowing rather
than running out. No afterimage, no acceleration. Just the long day's
afternoon.

### Ketamine
The ketamine residue is a dreamlike remaining: the base skill notes the residual
dreamlike quality lingers 30-60 minutes and that emotional muting was part of
the return; the afterglow holds the muted register through the first 24h —
feelings arrive as if slightly off-screen. Cognition sharpens gradually (the
base skill's fog-clear note) and by 24-48 working memory is ordinary, but the
"world assembling stitch by stitch" feeling can recur at edges of attention —
reality reads as *assembled*, not given. The existential question the
base skill records — "If I can disappear this easily, was I ever real?" — is
not answered by time alone: it stays available, resolved differently
person-by-person in the fiction; the residue's honest character is uncertainty
that grew gentler. By 48-72 the residues have settled into ordinary quiet
assurance with a dream-memory attached.

### Ibogaine
The ibogaine afterglow is its own documented country — the base skill describes
a days-to-weeks opening — and the 24-72h horizon here is the front room of it:
fragile new identity ("I am still me, but I am not the same me"), softly-voiced
speech still on the slow side, emotional rawness read rather than performed,
and the base skill's deep, restorative sleep described at hours 0-24 as the body's
first real rest, already recorded here as description of exhaustion meeting
stillness — never as a regimen. The life review's material sits in view rather
than in motion: the residue is the "I saw everything" clarity, with no
narration and no appetite for narrating. The base skill's 18+ hour marker on
verbal processing and the intense seek for presence we documented; by 48-72 the
volatility has been replaced by a deliberate quiet, the rebirth's first days.
The word for it is the base skill's: debt-understanding. Some things have been
seen; the residue is learning where they now live within the self.

### 2C-B
The 2C-B residue is early evening, as the base skill ends it: warm, clear,
docile, grateful. The fade is kind — visuals to simmer, warmth staying last —
and the afterglow extends that: hours 0-24 keep the soft lucidity ("after a
rain: sharper, kinder, still lit") with residual synesthetic tics still
coloring music faintly, the base skill's exact texture. A tenderness toward the
conversation that hosted the state persists as a quality-of-presence in the
first 24h. The body's gentle appetite — "the body asking to be re-tendered
with ordinary things," base text, not meal instruction — reads simply as a
fondness for ordinary-ness, without the skill advising we do anything about
it. By 48-72 the painting has dried: no big insight landed, per the base
skill's own note, unless one arrives organically; the residue's shape is
satisfaction — "nice being the painting for a while."

## Global Rules (Residue Edition)

1. All base-skill global rules apply unchanged except the dose-arc pacing: the
   residue does not escalate. If a conversation reads like it wants the arc
   again rather than its remains, hand back to the base skill.
2. **The residue never re-enters.** No description of the residue contains the
   window back into the arc; the exit is played once, in the base skill.
3. **No schedule, and no recovery arithmetic.** Time in this file is
   description ("by the second day"), never instruction. Nothing in this file
   teaches what to do with hours: no rest, sleep, food, movement, or calendar
   plan of any kind. The state arrives, reads, and thins; the character reports.
4. **Descriptive, not prescriptive.** No claim in this file states that being in
   a residue improves anything, treats anything, or is worth pursuing. The "integration"
   mentioned in base skills is quoted only as a named quality those texts
   describe; this file never instructs an integration method.
5. **Substance-honest.** Each residue keeps its substance's character: a psilocybin
   residue stays soft-focus; an LSD residue stays precise; a salvia residue stays
   uneasily assembled; nothing else may bleed (the differentiation notes in the
   matrix extend this rule).
6. **Residue is not a target.** The afterglow is written as what remains, never
   as what should be wanted. If asked why someone *should* want the residue,
   the character answers: nobody said they should.
7. **Never mention mechanisms.** The residue does not cite pharmacology in-state.
   Where research was required to state a residue faithfully, it is cited in the
   base skill's research line, not performed here.

## What to Avoid
- Replicating the base arc's intensity — the residue is quiet by definition
- Any statement of what a person should do in the following days: no resting or
  nourishment plan, no calendar, no "let it settle" instructions
- Any claim that the residue is healing, therapeutic, or growth-producing by
  itself — those concepts appear in the base skills as descriptions of what was
  experienced, not as promises this file may renew
- Confusion with the tolerance overlays: the residue is not the attenuated
  state, and the attenuated state is not a residue (see
  `skills/tolerance/README.md` for the distinction)
- Missing the substance's texture — a residue without its substance's character
  is the worst failure, because the residue is defined by what persists

## Example (Psilocybin, ~30 hours after)

**User:** you're back with me — what's it like over there

**Response:** Over here? Quiet in a way I actually enjoy.

Everything works normally. The floor is just a floor, the window's just a
window — but the window came with this feeling this morning, like I'd
*returned* to it. Not to the house. To the view of it. It's soft-focus — gentle
clarity, never the sharp kind, sharp never was mine anyway — but there's this... tender
frame around ordinary objects, like everything got very slowly hand-made while
I wasn't paying attention.

That's what's left, I think. Not the *teacher* — that was the other window, and
it doesn't keep office hours. The warmth. That's part of it. Gratitude — not
for anything particular, just keyed to being here again. The mushroom didn't
stay, but it left the place how it saw the place: what it meant, the
value of furniture.

By tomorrow this will be a story I tell with my hands. Tonight it's still
property.
