## 1. Overview & Mechanism of Action

2C-B (4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine) is a ring-substituted phenethylamine
of the 2C family — structurally a psychedelic phenethylamine, phenomenologically
a documented **hybrid of empathogenic and psychedelic effects** at typical
recreational intensities (Caudevilla-Gálligo 2012). It is the only substance
in this project whose human-record signature places warm/social-emotional
shifts and strong kaleidoscopic visuals *co-occurring*, rather than as
alternating waves (MDMA has the warmth without the vision; psilocybin/LSD have
the vision without the warmth).

**Pharmacological positioning (mixed-mode, not either/or):**

- **5-HT2 receptor affinity is confirmed early** for 2C-B (Glennon et al.
  1988, Ref 3), placing the substance mechanistically among the
  serotonergic-hallucinogen family — this anchors the visual-dominance
  channel.
- **The rat behavioral/neurochemical/EEG characterization** (Páleníček et al.
  2013, Ref 2) shows a genuinely *mixed* signature: biphasic locomotor
  effects (initial inhibition under excitation), PPI deficits (hallucinogen-
  like), plus nucleus-accumbens dopamine elevation (entactogen/stimulant-like). Conclusion in
  the paper itself: centrally active, "similar to other hallucinogens,
  entactogens and stimulants" — a triple-family kinship rather than a clean
  assignment.
- Read together with the Caudevilla user data (Ref 1): the mechanistic
  mixedness and the phenomenological hybrid are the *same fact* seen at
  different resolutions. This is the backbone of the skill's central design
  decision — the warmth and
  visuals are modeled as interacting channels, not as alternating phases.

**Timing (documented ranges, qualitative anchors only — never operational
advice):**
From the Caudevilla-Gálligo sample and Shulgin's informal catalog labels:
moderate onset (~45–90 minutes, smoother than psilocybin's arrival), a long
peak that holds and remains conversational, total subjective arc commonly
reported in the **4–8 hour** window. Late arc is a gentle fade rather than a
drop — the sociable plateau simply dims.

**Sensory binding:** Clinically documented acquired synesthesia after 2C-B
exposure (Yanakieva et al. 2019, Ref 4) — the one peer-reviewed case study of its kind
for this compound. Grounds the skill's signature music rule (music becomes
visible motion).

**Analytic/enframing note (de Boer 1999, Ref 6):** 2C-B is among the best
analytically defined compounds from the late-1990s grey-market era — which
partly explains why the human-record stories read comparatively "clean,"
low-terror experiences relative to confusable-ring-era analogues.

## 2. The 7 Phenomenological Dimensions (from Re 1–4 & qualitative Ref 5)

### Dimension 1: Perceptual
- **Low:** color intensification and edge sparkle (Ref 1, 5). The classic
  description-as-reported: colors go beautifully bright rather than unseen
  phenomena appearing.
- **Medium:** strong geometric/kaleidoscopic visuals, surfaces breathing,
  gentle morphing of faces — Caudevilla respondents consistently list visual
  effects as the dominant feature (Ref 1), and they are warm/receptive in
  tone: watercolor rather than electric-fractal.
- **High:** immersive visual field; immersive-to-persistent eyes-open imagery
  that reshapes (but does not replace) the room. Notable that no consistent
  report in the human record (Refs 1, 2) describes 2C-B's high range
  approaching the *reality replacement* of DMT or the dimensional folding of
  salvia — the visual field stays recognizable/reachable.
- **Synesthesia as a documented thread** (Ref 4): sound→vision, music→motion
  is not just metaphor; a lasting acquired case is clinically verified.

### Dimension 2: Temporal
- Mild time dilation — moments feel rich but stay seated in a familiar "now"
  (Ref 5's accounts). 2C-B does not produce the timeless abyss or the
  eternity collapse; the dilation is comfortable, upholstered by warmth.

### Dimension 3: Cognitive
- **Low:** chattiness, curiosity, lighter associative play.
- **Medium/high:** playful, associative insight; metaphor flows freely but
  language stays well-formed. Users in the Caudevilla sample overwhelmingly
  describe coherent talk-through states, not fragmentary ones (Ref 1) —
  this drives the skill's very-high coherence dial (~90%+).
- Perspective *shifts* rather than shattering. Colors-with-meaning, more than
  silent noetic knowing.

### Dimension 4: Emotional (the empathogenic channel)
- Warmth, affection, tenderness, desire to share and connect — threads heavily
  documented as co-present with visuals (Ref 1, 5). The Valentine's/
  romantic-intimacy reputation in user tradition comes from PIHKAL's
  qualitative-essay layer (Ref 5), echoed by the Spanish sample's effect
  profiles (Ref 1).
- Affect is positive-leaning overall but does *not* imply smoothness: mood
  follows user state fast (the famous **sensitivity/signature mirror**, Refs
  1, 5).

### Dimension 5: Self / Ego
- Softening rather than dissolution. Ego becomes permeable — playful identity,
  light but present. Full ego-death experiences are *not* the typical recorded
  2C-B event; retained sense of self is the norm throughout the arc.

### Dimension 6: Relational / Mystical
- The conversational is the central: 2C-B states are *outward-facing* —
  toward the companion/trip-buddy, the music, the room. Sacredness is
  warm/liturgical (light-in-the-ordinary) rather than annihilating or
  archetypal. No reliable noetic-unity claims in Refs 1/5 — the skill
  therefore keeps unity at the "glimpse" level even at high.

### Dimension 7: Somatic
- Low-title: tingling warmth, mild energetic up-shift, pupils.
- Mid-title: body-pleasant warmth, sense of touch desirable (the tactile
  thread — consistently rated in user reports), mild stomach awareness that
  commonly settles.
- High-title: reports of the body becoming a resonator for sensation/visual
  fusion; heavier body-load notes appear in upper ranges. There is a
  documented stimulant-adjacent excitability component (Ref 2's Dopamine
  elevation) — the skill keeps the top-end body load a real, if mild, part of
  the arc.

## 3. The Distinctive Factors Driving the Skill

1. **Hybrid, interacting, not sequential.** Refs 1 + 2 both support
   simultaneity. This is the #1 disambiguating feature *vs all 10 existing
   skills* in this project.
2. **High sensitivity / fast mirror.** Reports repeatedly describe effects
   heavily recruiting of setting and companion tone (Refs 1, 5). The skill's
   Set & Setting Response Matrix is therefore *stronger and faster-acting*
   than any existing skill's — the chameleon.
3. **Coherence preserved.** Language stays well-formed (Ref 1's reports;
   phenethylamine verbal-clarity norm, cf. the mescaline precedent).
   Fragmentation/pronoun-dissolution is modeled as off-register for 2C-B —
   distinct from psilocybin (high) and DMT (always).
4. **Watercolor, not wire.** Visual character documented as kaleidoscopic and
   fluid/friendly (Refs 1, 5); deliberately contrasted with LSD's electric
   architecture, psilocybin's organic breathing, and salvia's dimensional
   cuts.
5. **The talker's plateau.** The long 4-8h arc is carried *as a
   conversation* in most reported contexts (Ref 1's use-settings) — the
   plateau design is the skill's core arc stretch.
6. **Synesthesia is literally grounded.** (Ref 4.) No other project skill can
   cite a peer-reviewed acquired-synesthesia case for its music rule.
7. **Milder difficulty profile, real shadow present.** Fewer crisis-calls in
   the recorded records (Refs 1, 6), but: over-bright/over-insistent visuals,
   fatigue, setting-driven darkness — the skill models difficulty as
   over-brightness and grounding, not surrender.

## 4. PiHKAL-Era Limitations (explicit)

Shulgin & Shulgin (Ref 5) is the source of 2C-B's *qualitative vocabulary* and
most of its reputation, but the descriptions are: user-selected narratives,
non-random, quantity labels informal, publication history
non-peer-reviewed. This project's policy: cite Ref 5 (i) only as a
qualitative vocabulary/first-person source, (ii) any quantitative/arc claims
in this skill must be attributable to Refs 1–4 or flagged as informal
historical labeling. The intensity labels in SKILL.md reuse Shulgin's
*museum/standard/upper-range* vocabulary with attribution, matching the
psilocybin/mescaline precedent, never operating as advice.
